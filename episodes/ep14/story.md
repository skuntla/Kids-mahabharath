# Episode 14: Home to Hastinapura

*Last time, Madri called the Ashvini twins, and baby Nakula and baby Sahadeva were born. Now Pandu had five sons, the Pandavas! And far away in Hastinapura, Sage Vyasa helped Queen Gandhari, and one hundred and one babies came out of the pots: Duryodhana, his ninety-nine brothers, and little sister Dushala.*

Snuggle in close, little one. Tonight's story has a sad part, so hold my hand. But it has a happy ending too.

The five little Pandavas grew up in the mountain forest. The kind forest sages were their teachers. Yudhishthira learned to always tell the truth. Bhima grew big and strong. Arjuna loved his little bow. And the twins, Nakula and Sahadeva, followed their big brothers everywhere.

They climbed trees, splashed in streams and chased the deer. Can you **splash** like the Pandavas? **Splash, splash!**

*(pause)*

Pandu and his two queens, Kunti and Madri, watched them play and smiled. Those were happy, happy days.

*(long pause)*

Then, one spring, when the forest was full of flowers, something very sad happened. Remember Sage Kindama, who had said Pandu would not live long? His words came true. King Pandu became ill, and he passed away, gently, under the flowering trees.

Kunti and Madri cried and cried. The five boys cried too. Let's take a slow, soft breath together. **Breathe in... and out.**

*(long pause)*

Madri missed Pandu so much that she wanted to go with him, up to the stars. She held Kunti's hands.

"Kunti, my sister," she said softly, "please look after my Nakula and Sahadeva. Love them as your own."

"I will," said Kunti. "All five are my sons now." Madri smiled, and went with Pandu to the stars. Now they shine together in the night sky.

*(pause)*

Can you look up and find a twinkling star? Wave to it, gently. **Hello, star!**

*(long pause)*

The wise sages said, "Kunti, the boys must go home to Hastinapura. That is where they belong." So Kunti and the five boys set off, and the sages walked with them, all the long way.

Walk, walk, walk. Over the hills. **Step, step, step.** Across the rivers. **Splash, splash.** Through the deep green forest.

*(pause)*

At last, they saw the tall golden gates of Hastinapura! **Toot, toot!** went the conch shells. All the people came running to see.

Out came grandfather **Bhishma**, with his long white beard. Out came wise **Uncle Vidura**, and **King Dhritarashtra** with **Queen Gandhari**.

The oldest sage stepped forward. "These are the five sons of King Pandu," he said. "Please take care of them." Then, in a flash, the sages were gone, back to their forest!

*(pause)*

Bhishma opened his arms wide and hugged all five boys. Can you give a big hug, like Bhishma? **Hug!** Vidura took gentle care of Kunti, like a kind brother.

Grandmother Satyavati was very old now. Sage Vyasa told her, "Mother, come and rest in the peaceful forest." So Satyavati went to the forest with Ambika and Ambalika, to live quietly and pray.

*(long pause)*

And the five Pandavas? They had a new home, and a hundred cousins to meet!

**Tonight's lesson:** Kunti kept her promise and loved all five boys the same. When sad things happen, families hold each other close.

**A question for you:** Who is someone you love to hug when you feel sad?
