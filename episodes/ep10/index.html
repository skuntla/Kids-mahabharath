Kunti Chooses a King

Kids Mahabharath · Episode 10

For the grown-up: read slowly. Bold words are for acting out; · · · means a gentle pause. Pictures wait until the end!

Last time, kind Kunti looked after grumpy Sage Durvasa, and he gave her a secret prayer. She called Surya, the Sun God, and her baby, Karna, floated away in a basket, to be loved by Adhiratha and Radha.

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight, there is going to be a very special party!

Kunti grew up into a beautiful, gentle princess. One day, King Kuntibhoja said, "My dear Kunti, it is time for you to choose a husband. And you will choose him yourself!"

So he sent messengers to every kingdom. Toot, toot! went their conch shells. "Come, kings and princes! Come to the swayamvara!"

· · ·

A swayamvara is a special ceremony where the princess picks her husband, by putting a flower garland around his neck.

Can you blow a big conch shell? Toot, toooot!

· · ·

Kings came from far and near. Some had big moustaches. Some had shiny jewels. Some had very, very tall crowns! They all sat in a beautiful hall full of flowers.

And among them sat a young king from Hastinapura. He was tall and calm, with pale skin that seemed to glow like the moon, and a great bow on his shoulder.

Do you remember him? Yes! It was King Pandu, the wonderful archer!

· · · · ·

Kunti walked slowly down the hall, holding a big garland of orange marigolds and red roses. Step... step... step.

She looked at every king. And then, she saw Pandu. He was strong, but he was also kind and gentle. Kunti's heart said, "This is the one."

She lifted the garland, and swish! she placed it around Pandu's neck.

· · ·

Everyone clapped! Clap, clap, clap! Can you clap for Kunti and Pandu?

· · ·

So Kunti married King Pandu, and they rode in a golden chariot all the way to Hastinapura. Clip-clop, clip-clop!

At the palace gate, Grandfather Bhishma and Grandmother Satyavati were waiting, with flower petals and a little lamp. "Welcome home, Queen Kunti!"

· · · · ·

But wise Bhishma had another plan. In those days, a great king often had more than one queen. So Bhishma travelled to the kingdom of Madra, where the strong King Shalya lived.

King Shalya had a sister, a cheerful princess with jasmine flowers in her hair. Her name was Madri.

"Will Princess Madri marry our King Pandu?" asked Bhishma politely, with folded hands.

King Shalya smiled. "Yes! I am happy for my sister." And Madri smiled too.

· · ·

So Madri came to Hastinapura, and became Pandu's second queen. Now King Pandu had two queens, Kunti and Madri.

· · ·

Then King Pandu set out with his army, marching, marching, to faraway lands. He was so brave and so good with his bow that kingdom after kingdom became friends with Hastinapura, and sent gifts.

Can you march like Pandu's army? Left, right, left, right!

· · ·

When Pandu came home, he brought chests and chests of gold, jewels and silks. But he did not keep them all for himself. He shared them with his big brother Dhritarashtra, with Bhishma, and with his whole family.

Everyone in Hastinapura was happy and proud.

· · ·

Tonight's lesson: Kunti chose Pandu because he was kind, not just because he was strong. And Pandu shared his treasures, because good things feel even better when we share them.

A question for you: What is something you can share with someone tomorrow?

Next time, King Pandu goes hunting deep in the forest, and something surprising happens.

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
