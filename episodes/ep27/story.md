# Episode 27: Bhima and the Forest Giant

*Last time, the Pandavas escaped from the burning house through Uncle Vidura's secret tunnel. Bhima carried everyone through the night, and a kind boatman rowed them across the river Ganga.*

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight we go deep into the forest. Listen carefully... can you hear the owls? **Hoo, hoo!**

*(pause)*

The Pandavas walked and walked through the deep, dark forest. Their feet hurt. Their tummies rumbled. At last they came to a giant banyan tree.

"We are so thirsty," said Mother Kunti softly, and she sat down under the tree.

"I will find water," said Bhima. He followed the sound of frogs, **croak, croak**, until he found a cool pond. He soaked his shawl in the water and carried it all the way back.

But when he returned, everyone was already fast asleep on the ground.

*(pause)*

"My poor mother sleeps on the hard ground," Bhima thought sadly. "I will stay awake and keep them safe." So Bhima sat up, watching, all night long.

*(long pause)*

Now, not far away, on top of a tall tree, lived a huge forest giant called **Hidimba**. He had wild red hair, big yellow eyes, and two pointy tusks.

He lifted his big nose and **sniff, sniff, sniffed!** Can you sniff like a giant?

"I smell people!" he grumbled. "Sister Hidimbi, go and bring them to me!"

*(pause)*

So Hidimbi went through the trees. But when she saw Bhima, sitting so strong and brave beside his sleeping family, she stopped. "What a kind and handsome man," she thought. "I don't want my brother to hurt him."

Hidimbi had magic. She turned herself into a lovely young woman with flowers in her hair, and she walked up to Bhima.

"Please run away," she whispered. "My brother Hidimba is coming, and he is very angry!"

Bhima smiled. "Thank you," he said. "But I will not leave my family. And I am not afraid."

*(long pause)*

Just then, the trees went **crash, crash, CRASH!** Big Hidimba came stomping through the forest. **Stomp, stomp, stomp!**

"Sister! Why are you talking to him?" he roared.

Bhima stood up. "Shh!" he said. "Don't wake my mother. If you want to fight, fight me, far from here!"

Bhima grabbed the giant's arms and pulled him away from the sleeping family. They pushed and pulled. They wrestled and rolled. Trees shook, and birds flew up into the sky. **Whoosh, flap, flap!**

*(pause)*

The noise woke up Mother Kunti and the brothers. "Bhima!" called Arjuna. "Do you need help?"

"No, brother," laughed Bhima. "Watch this!" With one mighty **heave**, Bhima lifted the giant high and threw him down. **THUD!** Hidimba was beaten, and he could never trouble anyone in that forest again.

*(long pause)*

Then Hidimbi came forward and folded her hands before Mother Kunti. "I tried to warn your son," she said. "He is so brave and so good. May I marry him?"

Mother Kunti looked at Yudhishthira. Yudhishthira looked at Bhima. And Bhima smiled the biggest smile you ever saw.

*(pause)*

**Tonight's lesson:** Bhima stayed awake to keep his family safe, and he was brave without being afraid. And Hidimbi chose to be kind, even when her own brother wanted her to be mean. We can always choose kindness too.

**A question for you:** If you kept watch over your family at night, what would you listen for?
