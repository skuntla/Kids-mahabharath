The Deer in the Forest

Kids Mahabharath · Episode 11

For the grown-up: read slowly. Bold words are for acting out; · · · means a gentle pause. Pictures wait until the end!

Last time, Kunti chose kind King Pandu at her swayamvara, and Madri became his second queen. Brave Pandu won many lands, and shared all his treasures with his family.

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight, we are going deep, deep into the forest.

After all his marching, King Pandu felt tired. "I would love some peace and quiet," he said. "Let us go and live in the forest for a while."

So King Pandu, Queen Kunti and Queen Madri left the busy palace, and went to the big, green forest near the Himalaya mountains.

· · ·

The forest was full of sounds. Birds sang, tweet, tweet! Monkeys chattered, ooh-ooh, aah-aah! And the wind went whoooosh through the tall trees.

Can you be a monkey swinging from a branch? Ooh-ooh, aah-aah!

· · ·

In those days, kings went hunting with bows and arrows. One morning, Pandu went walking quietly through the trees. Tiptoe, tiptoe.

Then he saw them. In a sunny meadow, two beautiful deer were playing together, nuzzling each other, very happy.

Pandu lifted his great bow. He pulled back the string. Stretch... and... twang!

· · · · ·

The arrow flew, and it hit one of the deer.

But then, something magical and surprising happened. The deer began to glow, and it changed... into a man! An old sage with a white beard!

His name was Sage Kindama. He and his wife had turned themselves into deer, so they could play happily in the forest.

· · · · ·

The sage looked at Pandu with sad eyes. "Oh King, why did you hurt us, when we were so happy together, and doing no harm?"

Pandu's heart felt heavy. He dropped his bow. Thud. "I am so sorry," he whispered. "I did not know."

· · ·

Then the sage said some very serious words. "Because you hurt us when we were happy together, you will never be able to have little children in the usual way."

And then the sage closed his eyes, and his life went gently up into the sky, like a soft light.

· · · · ·

Pandu sat down under a tree and cried. Can you show a sad face, like Pandu? Now take a big, slow breath with him. Breathe in... and out.

He thought and thought. Then he said, "I made a big mistake. I will not be a hunting king any more. I will live simply in the forest, and be gentle to every living thing, like a sage."

· · ·

Pandu sent a messenger galloping back to Hastinapura. Clip-clop, clip-clop! He sent his crown and his jewels to his big brother, Dhritarashtra. "Please look after our kingdom for me," said his message.

So blind Dhritarashtra and Gandhari now ruled Hastinapura, with wise Bhishma and Vidura beside them.

· · ·

Kunti and Madri took Pandu's hands. "We will stay with you, wherever you go," they said.

So the three of them lived in a little hut on a mountain, with kind sages as their friends. They ate fruits, prayed by the river, and were kind to every bird and deer.

But Pandu was still a little sad. He wished for children so much.

· · ·

Tonight's lesson: Even kings make mistakes. When Pandu made one, he said sorry, and he changed the way he lived. Saying sorry and doing better makes us truly brave.

A question for you: Can you think of a time you said sorry? How did it feel?

Next time, Kunti remembers her secret prayer, and something wonderful happens in the forest.

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
