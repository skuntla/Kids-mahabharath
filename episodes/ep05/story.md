# Episode 5: Amba's Brave Journey

*Last time, Bhishma brought home three princesses from Kashi. Princess Amba told him she had already chosen King Shalva, so Bhishma kindly let her go to him.*

Snuggle in, little one. Let's see what happened next.

Princess Amba travelled a long way to the city of King Shalva. Her heart went **thump, thump, thump**, she was so excited. "King Shalva," she said, "I have come back to you!"

But King Shalva folded his arms and turned away. "Bhishma won you in front of all the kings," he said proudly. "Go back to him. I will not marry you now."

*(pause)*

Poor Amba! Her eyes filled with tears. **Sniff, sniff.** Can you make a sad face, like Amba?

So Amba went all the way back to Hastinapura. "Bhishma," she said, "King Shalva sent me away. You brought me here, so now you must marry me."

Bhishma's face was sad, too. "Princess, I am sorry," he said gently. "Long ago, I made a great promise never to marry. I cannot break my promise."

*(pause)*

Amba walked out of the palace and into the deep, quiet forest. The birds went **tweet, tweet**, and the leaves went **swish, swish**. There she found a little hut where wise sages lived.

And guess who was there? An old, gentle sage named **Hotravahana**. He was Amba's very own grandfather! He gave her a big, warm hug. "Don't cry, my dear," he said. "I know someone who can help you. His name is **Parashurama**."

*(pause)*

Parashurama was a mighty sage who carried a big axe and a great bow. And here is a secret: long ago, he had been **Bhishma's teacher**! He had taught Bhishma how to shoot arrows.

Parashurama listened to Amba's story. Then he said, "Come, Princess. Bhishma always listens to his teacher."

*(pause)*

Parashurama went to the field near Hastinapura and called out, "**Bhishma!**" Bhishma came running and bowed down low to touch his teacher's feet. "Welcome, my Guru."

"Bhishma," said Parashurama, "marry Princess Amba."

Bhishma folded his hands. "My Guru, I love you and respect you. But I made a promise to my father. I can never break it, not even for you."

"Then," said Parashurama, lifting his bow, "you must fight me!"

*(long pause)*

And so the teacher and the student had a great, great battle. **Twang! Whoosh! Boom!** Arrows filled the sky like a flock of birds. They fought for days and days, and neither one could win. The whole world began to shake, **rumble, rumble!**

*(pause)*

Then, a soft, shining light appeared. It was **Ganga**, the river goddess, Bhishma's mother! And the wise gods came, too. "Please stop," they said. "You are both too strong. If you keep fighting, the whole world will be hurt."

Parashurama lowered his bow. Bhishma lowered his bow. And Parashurama smiled. "Bhishma, you are a wonderful student. You keep your promises, no matter what."

*(pause)*

Parashurama turned to Amba. "I am sorry, Princess. I tried my very best."

Amba wiped her tears. She stood up tall and said, "Thank you, Parashurama. I will not give up. I will go and pray to the great god **Shiva** for help."

And brave Amba walked away, all alone, toward the tall snowy mountains.

*(pause)*

**Tonight's lesson:** Even when things feel very sad, we can be brave and keep trying. And keeping our promises is important, too.

**A question for you:** Have you ever felt sad, and then found the courage to try again?
