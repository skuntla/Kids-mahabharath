Drona's Guru-Dakshina

Kids Mahabharath · Episode 23

For the grown-up: read slowly. Bold words are for acting out; · · · means a gentle pause. Pictures wait until the end!

Last time, Karna did every trick Arjuna could do, and Duryodhana made him King of Anga. Karna touched the feet of his old father Adhiratha, and Karna and Duryodhana became best friends forever.

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight the princes give their teacher a very special gift!

When the princes had finished learning, Guru Drona called them all together under a big tree.

"My dear students," he said, "a student always gives his teacher a thank-you gift. It is called guru-dakshina. I don't want gold. I don't want jewels."

· · ·

"Far away is the kingdom of Panchala. Its king is Drupada. When we were boys, we studied together, and he promised to share his kingdom with me. But when I went to him, poor and hungry, he laughed and said, 'A king can never be friends with a poor man!'"

Drona stroked his grey beard. "For my gift, bring King Drupada to me. Bring him safely. Do not hurt him."

· · ·

Duryodhana jumped up first. "We'll do it!" Off went the Kauravas, with Karna too, their chariots going rumble, rumble, rumble.

But Drupada's soldiers were ready. Arrows came down like rain, whoosh, whoosh, whoosh! The Kauravas had to turn their chariots around and hurry back home. Can you turn your chariot around, quick as you can?

· · · · ·

Then Arjuna folded his hands. "Gurudev, now it is our turn."

The Pandavas rode out. Big Bhima ran in front, swinging his heavy mace, boom, boom! Soldiers scattered like pigeons, and chariot wheels rolled away.

Bhima wanted to keep going, but Arjuna called out, "Stop, brother! These are the people of our teacher's old friend. We came only for the king."

· · ·

Arjuna's arrows went twang, twang! One arrow cut the string of Drupada's bow. Then, with a mighty leap, hup!, Arjuna jumped right onto the king's chariot and held him tight. Can you do a big jump like Arjuna? Hup!

· · ·

The Pandavas brought King Drupada to Guru Drona, gently, without a scratch.

Drona smiled a small smile. "Hello, old friend. Do you remember me? You said a king can only be friends with a king. So now I will take half of your kingdom. I will rule the north side of the river, and you rule the south side. Now we are both kings. Shall we be friends again?"

· · · · ·

Drupada bowed his head. "Yes, we will be friends." And Drona let him go home, free.

But on the long ride home, Drupada's heart was still upset. He looked at the sky and made a wish. "One day, I want a son strong enough to win against Drona, and a daughter good enough to marry a hero like Arjuna."

Hmm. Will his wish come true? We will find out!

· · ·

Back home, Guru Drona put his hand on Arjuna's head. "You gave me the best gift a student could give." And Arjuna's heart felt warm and happy.

· · · · ·

Tonight's lesson: A promise is a precious thing. When we make a promise to a friend, we should keep it, even when we grow big and important.

A question for you: What is a thank-you gift you could give to your teacher?

Next time, a king's wish brings two amazing children out of a holy fire: The Children of the Fire.

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
