# Episode 13: Madri's Twins and Gandhari's Hundred

*Last time, Kunti used Sage Durvasa's secret prayer. Dharma, the god of goodness, gave her baby Yudhishthira. Vayu, the wind god, gave her strong baby Bhima, who broke a rock. And Indra, king of the gods, gave her brave baby Arjuna. But gentle Madri was wishing for a baby of her own.*

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight, there are babies, babies, and more babies!

One quiet morning, Madri sat by the river, looking a little sad. Pandu sat beside her. "What is wrong, my dear?" he asked.

"Kunti has three little sons," Madri said softly. "I wish I could have a baby too."

*(pause)*

So Pandu went to Kunti. "Kunti," he said, "will you share your secret prayer with Madri, just once?"

Kunti smiled a big, kind smile. "Of course!" she said. Can you make a big, kind smile, like Kunti? **Show me!**

*(long pause)*

Kunti taught Madri the secret words. Madri thought very carefully. "Which god should I call?" she wondered. Then her eyes sparkled. "I will call two gods at once!"

She closed her eyes and whispered the prayer. **Shhh...**

**Clip-clop, clip-clop!** A golden chariot came down from the sky, pulled by shining horses. In it were the **Ashvini twins**, the two gods of healing, who look exactly alike.

*(pause)*

They blessed Madri, and soon, not one but **two** baby boys were born! Twins! They named them **Nakula** and **Sahadeva**.

Nakula would grow up to be the most handsome prince, and wonderful with horses. Sahadeva would grow up to be very, very wise, and know about the stars.

Now Pandu had five sons. Let's count them on our fingers! **Yudhishthira, Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula, Sahadeva.** One, two, three, four, five! They are called the **Pandavas**.

*(pause)*

Now, far away in the palace of Hastinapura, Queen Gandhari had been waiting and waiting for her babies. Remember, Shiva had promised her a hundred children!

She waited one year. **Tick, tock.** She waited two years! **Tick, tock, tick, tock.** But still no babies came. When she heard that Kunti had a baby boy already, Gandhari felt sad and tired of waiting.

*(long pause)*

Then, something strange came instead of a baby: a hard, round ball, cold like a stone. Gandhari was so upset!

But wise **Sage Vyasa** came hurrying to the palace. "Do not worry, Queen Gandhari," he said gently. "Shiva's blessing will come true."

*(pause)*

Vyasa sprinkled cool water on the ball, and **pop!** It broke into one hundred and one tiny pieces. He asked the servants to bring one hundred and one big pots, filled with ghee. Each tiny piece went into its own pot, and the pots were kept safe and warm.

Can you count to five, like counting pots? **One, two, three, four, five!** Phew! Vyasa had to count all the way to one hundred and one!

*(long pause)*

After a long time, the first pot opened. Out came a baby boy! He was named **Duryodhana**, the eldest. And he was born on the very same day as strong little Bhima, far away in the forest.

Then, one by one, the other pots opened. **Waaah! Waaah!** Ninety-nine more baby brothers! And from the last pot came one little baby girl, **Dushala**.

They are called the **Kauravas**. The palace was full of crying, giggling babies!

*(pause)*

**Tonight's lesson:** Kunti shared her prayer with Madri, and Gandhari learned to be patient. Sharing and waiting patiently both bring good things.

**A question for you:** If you had ninety-nine brothers, what game would you all play together?
