Kunti's Secret Blessing

Kids Mahabharath · Episode 9

For the grown-up: read slowly. Bold words are for acting out; · · · means a gentle pause. Pictures wait until the end!

Last time, King Pandu was crowned, and Prince Dhritarashtra married the gentle Princess Gandhari, who tied a soft blindfold over her eyes to share everything with him.

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight, we meet a brand new princess!

Long ago, a king named Shurasena had a little daughter called Pritha. His dear cousin, King Kuntibhoja, had no children at all. So Shurasena gave little Pritha to him, as a very special gift.

King Kuntibhoja hugged her tight, and he gave her a new name: Kunti.

· · ·

Kunti grew up kind, and helpful, and very curious.

One day, a famous sage came to the palace. His name was Durvasa. He had wild white hair, and everyone whispered, "Shhh! Be careful! If Sage Durvasa gets cross, he grumbles and stomps!"

· · ·

Can you make a grumpy face, like Sage Durvasa? Now stomp, stomp, stomp!

· · ·

"Kunti," said the king, "please look after our guest."

So Kunti did. She brought him warm food, yum. She lit his little lamp each night, flick. She woke up before the sun to fetch fresh water, splash, splash. And she never, ever complained.

Days became months. And Sage Durvasa never got cross even once!

· · · · ·

When it was time to leave, Durvasa smiled at Kunti. "You have served me beautifully," he said. "So I will give you a secret blessing."

He leaned close and whispered a magic prayer in her ear.

"With this prayer, you can call any god you like. And that god will give you a baby."

· · ·

Can you whisper a secret to me, very, very softly?

· · ·

Kunti was still a young girl. One morning, she looked out of her window at the bright, golden sun. And she wondered, "Does the magic prayer really work?"

She closed her eyes and whispered it.

Whoosh! The room filled with golden light. And there stood Surya, the Sun God himself, shining like a thousand lamps!

· · ·

Kunti got a big fright. "Oh no! I was only trying it!" she said.

But Surya said gently, "A blessing from a great sage must come true." And soon, Kunti had a beautiful baby boy.

He was born wearing shining golden armour on his chest and golden earrings, glowing just like his father, the Sun.

· · · · ·

But Kunti was frightened. She was not married yet. What would everyone say?

So, with tears in her eyes, she laid her baby in a soft basket, and put the basket on the river. "Please keep him safe," she whispered. Bob, bob went the basket, floating far away.

· · ·

Far down the river, a kind chariot driver named Adhiratha saw something shining in the water. He pulled out the basket and called to his wife, Radha.

"Look! A baby, with golden armour!"

Radha had always wished for a child. She held him close and smiled. "He is our son now."

They named him Vasusena. But one day, the whole world would know him by another name: Karna.

· · ·

Tonight's lesson: Kunti was rewarded because she was patient and kind, even to a grumpy guest. And special gifts, like magic prayers, should be used carefully, not just to see what happens.

A question for you: Who is someone you can be extra kind to tomorrow?

Next time, Princess Kunti grows up and chooses a husband, at a grand flower-garland ceremony!

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
