The Great Show of Skills

Kids Mahabharath · Episode 21

For the grown-up: read slowly. Bold words are for acting out; · · · means a gentle pause. Pictures wait until the end!

Last time, Ekalavya, the brave forest boy, gave Guru Drona the hardest thank-you gift of all, his right thumb, with a smiling heart. Drona blessed him, and Arjuna promised to practice harder than ever.

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight there will be drums, flags and lots of cheering!

· · ·

Many years passed. The little princes grew taller and stronger, until they were almost grown up. One day Guru Drona went to King Dhritarashtra and said, "Your Majesty, the princes have finished their training. Let us show the whole kingdom what they can do!"

So the king's workers built a giant arena, a big round playground with tall seats all around it. Colourful flags waved in the wind. And on the big day, the drums went BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Can you drum on your knees? BOOM! BOOM!

· · ·

Everyone came. King Dhritarashtra could not see, so wise Vidura sat beside him and told him everything. Queen Gandhari wore her blindfold, so Kunti sat beside her and whispered what was happening.

In walked Guru Drona in his plain white clothes, with his son Ashwatthama. Then came the princes, one by one, and the crowd went "Ooooh!"

Yudhishthira raced his chariot round and round. Clip-clop, clip-clop! Nakula and Sahadeva swung their shining swords. Swish! Swish!

· · ·

Then Bhima and Duryodhana stepped in with their heavy maces, big iron clubs. CLANG! CLANG! But this was only supposed to be a show, and the two cousins started to get angry. CLANG! The crowd started to shout, "Bhima!" "No, Duryodhana!"

Guru Drona said, "Ashwatthama, stop them, quickly!" And Ashwatthama ran between them. "Enough! This is a show, not a fight!" Slowly, the two cousins lowered their maces, still frowning.

· · · · ·

Then Drona raised his hand, and everyone went quiet. "Now meet my dearest student, Arjuna!"

Arjuna walked in with his bow. Kunti's eyes filled with happy tears.

Twang! He shot an arrow, and it burst into fire. Twang! Another arrow, and it rained cool water to put the fire out. Twang! One more, and a big wind went whoooosh, making everyone's clothes flap! Can you blow like the wind? Whoooosh!

· · ·

Then the helpers swung a cow's horn on a rope, back and forth, back and forth. Arjuna shot twenty-one arrows, twang-twang-twang, and every single one landed inside the swinging horn!

The crowd jumped up and cheered. "Arjuna! Arjuna!" Drona smiled the biggest smile.

· · · · ·

And just then, at the big gate, there was a sound like thunder. CLAP! CLAP! Someone was slapping his strong arms, the way wrestlers do.

Everyone turned around. There stood a tall young man. On his chest shone golden armour, and in his ears hung golden earrings. He glowed like the morning sun.

Kunti held her breath. Her heart went thump, thump. She knew that golden armour. But she did not say a word.

· · · · ·

Tonight's lesson: We practice to do our best, not to beat others. When we play, we stay friends, even when we want to win.

A question for you: Who do you think the shining young man is? Do you remember a baby with golden armour floating in a basket?

Next time, the golden stranger has a big challenge for Arjuna: Karna, King of Anga.

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
