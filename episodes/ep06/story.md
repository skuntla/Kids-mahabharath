# Episode 6: Amba's Wish

*Last time, King Shalva and Bhishma both said no to Princess Amba. Even Bhishma's teacher, Parashurama, could not help her. So brave Amba walked toward the snowy mountains to pray to the great god Shiva.*

Snuggle in, little one. Let's see what happened next.

High up in the mountains, the wind went **whoooo, whoooo**. The snow was cold and white. Amba sat down under a tall pine tree, closed her eyes, and began to pray.

"Om Namah Shivaya," she whispered. "Om Namah Shivaya."

*(pause)*

Can you sit very still, like Amba? Close your eyes. **Shhh.** Breathe in, and breathe out.

Amba prayed and prayed. She ate only fruits and leaves. Then only a little water. The snow fell, and melted. The flowers bloomed, and faded. Summer came, and winter came, again and again. And still Amba prayed.

*(pause)*

The animals of the forest came to watch her. A little deer. A fluffy rabbit. A big snow bear. They had never seen anyone so patient!

Then one day, the sky began to glow. **Shimmer, shimmer!** The ground went **rumble, rumble**.

*(pause)*

And there, in a bright blue light, stood the great god **Shiva**! He had a crescent moon in his long hair, a snake curled gently around his neck, and a trident in his hand. His eyes were kind.

"Amba," said Shiva, in a deep, soft voice. "You have prayed for so long. What is your wish?"

*(long pause)*

Amba folded her hands. "Lord Shiva, nobody would help me. Please let me be strong enough to face Bhishma one day."

Shiva smiled. "Your wish will come true, Amba. But not in this life. You will be born again as a new child. And in a great battle, far, far in the future, you will stand in front of Bhishma. Because of you, Bhishma will put down his bow."

*(pause)*

Amba bowed down low. "Thank you, Lord Shiva!" Her heart felt light, like a feather.

Then Amba looked up at the stars. She knew her wish would come true in her next life. She closed her eyes, and her spirit rose up, up, up, like a tiny shining spark, flying into the night sky, ready to be born again.

*(pause)*

Now, far away, there was a kingdom called **Panchala**, ruled by a good king named **Drupada**. King Drupada and his queen had been praying to Lord Shiva for a child.

One morning, the palace bells went **ding, dong, ding!** A baby was born! King Drupada held the tiny baby and laughed with joy. They named the baby **Shikhandi**.

*(pause)*

Shikhandi grew up brave and strong. Shikhandi loved to ride horses, **clip-clop, clip-clop**, and to practise with a bow, **twang!** And deep, deep inside, Shikhandi carried a little bit of brave Amba's heart.

*(pause)*

And Bhishma? Back in Hastinapura, Bhishma stood on the palace roof and looked at the stars. He remembered Princess Amba and felt a little sad. But he still kept his promise, every single day.

*(pause)*

**Tonight's lesson:** Being patient is a kind of strength. When we keep trying, gently and bravely, good things can happen, even if we have to wait a long time.

**A question for you:** What is something you are waiting for, and can you be patient like Amba?
