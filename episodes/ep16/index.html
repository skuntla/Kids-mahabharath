Bhima and the Snake Kingdom

Kids Mahabharath · Episode 16

For the grown-up: read slowly. Bold words are for acting out; · · · means a gentle pause. Pictures wait until the end!

Last time, the five Pandavas and the hundred Kaurava cousins played together in Hastinapura. Bhima won every race, splashed his cousins in the pond and shook them out of the mango tree. Duryodhana frowned, and a little seed of jealousy grew in his heart. Mother Kunti taught Bhima that strength is for helping.

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight we go somewhere very surprising... deep, deep under the river!

Duryodhana's jealous seed grew and grew. One day he whispered to Dushasana, "Let's have a big picnic by the river Ganga. And I have a sneaky plan for Bhima."

· · ·

So all the cousins went to a lovely garden by the river, with tents of bright cloth. There were games, and swimming, and plates and plates of sweets! Bhima loved sweets. Yum, yum, yum! Can you rub your tummy like Bhima?

But Duryodhana had hidden something bad in Bhima's sweets, to make him very, very sleepy. Bhima ate them all up. Then he played in the water until he was tired. Yaaawn!

· · · · ·

Bhima lay down on the riverbank and fell fast asleep. Snore... snore...

Then Duryodhana did a very unkind thing. He tied Bhima with long vines, and pushed him into the river! Splooosh! Down, down, down Bhima sank, all the way to the bottom of the deep river.

· · · · ·

But at the bottom of the river was a hidden kingdom: the kingdom of the Nagas, the snake people! Their palaces glowed with shining jewels.

Some snake guards saw a big boy sinking down. "Who is this?" they hissed. Hissss! They gave him little bites. But guess what? The snake bites chased the bad sleepy stuff right out of Bhima's body!

Bhima opened his eyes. Snap! He broke the vines like thread. Can you break free like Bhima? Snap, snap!

· · ·

The snakes took Bhima to their king, Vasuki, a great shining snake with many heads and a jewelled crown.

Beside him was a very old snake named Aryaka. He looked closely at Bhima and smiled. "Why, this boy is Kunti's son! He is family! Kunti's father was my own grandson!"

King Vasuki was happy. "Then let us give him a special gift."

· · · · ·

They brought out bowls of a magic drink, glowing like gold. Each bowl gave the strength of a thousand elephants! Bhima drank one bowl... two... three... all the way to eight bowls! Can you count to eight with me? One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight! Gulp!

Then Bhima fell into a long, cosy sleep in the snake palace, for eight whole days.

· · · · ·

Up in Hastinapura, Mother Kunti was so worried. "Where is my Bhima?" Wise Uncle Vidura held her hand. "Do not fear, Kunti. Bhima is strong. He will come back."

· · ·

On the ninth day, Bhima woke up, stronger than ever before! The kind snakes carried him up, up, up to the riverbank. Bhima ran all the way home. Thump, thump, thump!

"Mother! I'm back!" Kunti hugged him tight, and his four brothers jumped on him with happy shouts.

Bhima told them everything. Uncle Vidura said quietly, "Let us keep this a secret, and always stay together and careful."

· · · · ·

Tonight's lesson: Jealousy can make people do unkind things, but kindness comes back to us in surprising ways. And family is always there to help, even under the river!

A question for you: If you were in the snake kingdom, what would you want to see?

Next time, the princes get their very first teacher, wise Guru Kripa, and then a great archery teacher arrives... Drona!

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
