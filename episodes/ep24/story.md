# Episode 24: The Children of the Fire

*Last time, the Pandavas brought King Drupada to Guru Drona, and Drona took half of his kingdom. On the long ride home, Drupada made a wish: a son strong enough to win against Drona, and a daughter good enough for a hero like Arjuna.*

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight, something truly magical happens!

King Drupada could not stop thinking about his wish. He walked by the river Ganga, up and down, up and down, looking for wise sages who could help him.

*(pause)*

At last he found two brothers living in a quiet forest hut. Their names were **Yaja** and **Upayaja**.

First Drupada asked the younger brother, Upayaja. Upayaja shook his head. "No, no." But Drupada did not give up. He came back again and again and served him kindly for a whole year.

Finally Upayaja said, "Go and ask my big brother, Yaja. He will help you."

*(pause)*

So Yaja and Upayaja came to Drupada's palace to do a great fire prayer, called a **yagna**. They built a square altar of bricks and lit a big fire. The flames went **crackle, crackle, crackle!**

The sages sang holy words, "**Om… Swaha!**" and poured spoonfuls of ghee into the fire. Each time, the fire jumped higher, **whoosh!** Can you make your hands into dancing flames, going up, up, up?

*(long pause)*

Then something amazing happened.

Out of the middle of the fire stepped a young man! He was bright like the flames. He wore a shining crown and golden armour, and he held a sword and a bow. He even had a chariot ready! He did not get burnt at all.

A voice from the sky said, "**This boy is born to win against Drona!**"

The young man was named **Dhrishtadyumna**. That's a big name! Let's say it slowly together: **Dhrish… ta… dyum… na.**

*(pause)*

But the fire was not finished yet.

From the altar rose a young woman, glowing softly. Her skin was dark and beautiful, like a rain cloud. Her eyes were like lotus petals, and her long black hair was curly. And all around her, the air smelled sweet, like blue lotus flowers. Take a big sniff... **mmmm!**

*(pause)*

The voice from the sky spoke again: "**She is the best of all women. She will help change the whole world.**"

Because her skin was dark, they called her **Krishnaa**. Because her father was King Drupada, she was also called **Draupadi**. And because she came from the land of Panchala, people called her **Panchali** too. One girl, three names!

*(long pause)*

King Drupada's eyes filled with happy tears. He hugged his new son and his new daughter. And their sibling **Shikhandi** came running to meet them too. What a big family now!

*(pause)*

And now, here is the most surprising part. When Dhrishtadyumna grew up a little, Guru Drona himself agreed to teach him archery. Drona knew about the voice from the sky, but a good teacher never says no to a student who comes to learn.

*(long pause)*

**Tonight's lesson:** Drupada did not give up, even when the answer was "no." When something is important, we keep trying patiently and kindly.

**A question for you:** If you could make a wish, what would it be?
