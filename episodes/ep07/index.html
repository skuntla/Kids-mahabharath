Three Little Princes

Kids Mahabharath · Episode 7

For the grown-up: read slowly. Bold words are for acting out; · · · means a gentle pause. Pictures wait until the end!

Last time, brave Amba prayed to Lord Shiva in the snowy mountains, and she was born again as Shikhandi, the child of King Drupada. And Bhishma still kept his promise, every single day.

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight we go back to the palace of Hastinapura.

King Vichitravirya lived happily with his two queens, Ambika and Ambalika. But one day, the young king became very, very sick. The doctors tried and tried. Bhishma sat by his little brother's bed and held his hand. But the king grew weaker, and weaker, and then he passed away.

· · ·

The whole palace was quiet. Sniff, sniff. Everyone was sad. Now there was no king, and no little prince to be king one day.

Queen Satyavati wiped her tears. "Bhishma," she said, "please, you be the king now. Please get married and have children."

Bhishma folded his hands. "Mother, I love you. But I made a promise. I will never be king, and I will never marry. I cannot break my promise."

· · ·

Can you hold up one finger and say, "A promise is a promise"?

Satyavati thought and thought. Then she smiled a little. "Long ago, before I came to the palace, I had a son. He is a great, wise sage named Vyasa. He lives in the forest. I will call him!"

· · ·

Satyavati closed her eyes and thought of her son. And whoosh! Sage Vyasa came. He had long, tangled hair, a big dark beard, and he smelled of the forest after rain. He had walked a long, long way.

"Mother, I am here," said Vyasa. "How can I help?"

"Please bless our family with children," said Satyavati.

Vyasa nodded. "I will bless the queens. They will each have a baby."

· · · · ·

But Sage Vyasa looked very wild after his long journey. When Queen Ambika saw him, she got scared and squeezed her eyes shut, like this! Can you shut your eyes tight?

· · ·

When Queen Ambalika saw him, she was so scared that her face turned pale, all white, like milk!

And there was a kind and gentle helper in the palace, a maid. She was not scared at all. She greeted the sage with a smile and a namaste.

· · ·

And so, three baby boys were born.

Queen Ambika's baby was named Dhritarashtra. He was big and very strong, as strong as a hundred elephants! But because his mother had shut her eyes, he was born blind. He could not see.

Queen Ambalika's baby was named Pandu. Because his mother had turned pale, Pandu's skin was pale too. He grew up to be a wonderful archer. Twang!

And the kind helper's baby was named Vidura. Vidura was the wisest of all. He always knew what was fair and right.

· · ·

The palace bells went ding, dong, ding! The sadness went away, and everyone smiled again.

And who do you think looked after the three little princes? Uncle Bhishma! He taught them to ride horses, clip-clop, to be strong, and to always be good. He loved them very, very much.

· · ·

Tonight's lesson: Everyone has their own special gift. Dhritarashtra was strong, Pandu was a great archer, and Vidura was wise. We don't all need to be the same.

A question for you: What is your special gift?

Next time, we will see the three princes grow up, and meet a princess who wears a blindfold.

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
