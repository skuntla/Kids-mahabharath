# Episode 3: Big Brother Bhishma

*Last time, Devavrata made a great and mighty promise: he would never be king, and he would always protect the kingdom. Flowers fell from the sky, and from that day he was called Bhishma.*

Snuggle in, little one. Let's see what happened next.

King Shantanu and Queen Satyavati lived happily in the palace of Hastinapura. And soon, there was wonderful news. A baby boy was born! They named him **Chitrangada**. *(pause)* Then, a little while later, another baby boy was born! They named him **Vichitravirya**. **Waah, waah!** went the two little babies.

Bhishma looked at his tiny brothers and smiled the biggest smile. "I will look after you," he whispered, "always."

*(pause)*

The two princes grew bigger and bigger. Big brother Bhishma taught them everything. He showed them how to hold a bow and **pull the string, and let go. Whoosh!** He taught them to ride horses, **clip-clop, clip-clop**, and to be fair and kind to everyone.

Chitrangada was bold and brave and loved adventures. Little Vichitravirya was gentle, and he loved to sit close to Bhishma and listen to stories.

*(pause)*

Years went by, and King Shantanu grew very old. His hair became white as clouds. One quiet evening, he called his family close. He held Satyavati's hand and looked at Bhishma with love. "My son, please take care of everyone," he said softly.

"I will, Father," said Bhishma. "I promise."

And then King Shantanu closed his eyes, and went peacefully to the heavens, to rest among the stars. *(long pause)*

Everyone missed him very much. When we miss someone, it is all right to feel sad. Satyavati and the boys cried, and Bhishma **hugged them all tight**.

*(pause)*

Now who would be the new king? Remember Bhishma's promise? He would never be king. So Bhishma placed the crown on Chitrangada's head. "Long live **King Chitrangada**!" everyone cheered.

Chitrangada was a strong king. But one day, a mighty sky-warrior from the clouds, a **Gandharva**, came down. And guess what? He had the very same name, Chitrangada! "There cannot be two Chitrangadas," said the sky-warrior. "Come and fight me!"

Brave King Chitrangada rode out in his chariot, **rumble, rumble**. They fought for a very long time, near a river, far away. *(pause)* And sadly, King Chitrangada did not come home.

*(long pause)*

Now only little Vichitravirya was left, and he was still just a boy. "Am I the king now?" he asked in a small voice.

Bhishma knelt down and held his hand. "Yes, little brother. You are the king. And I will stand beside you and protect you, until you are grown up and strong."

So Bhishma helped Queen Satyavati look after the whole kingdom. He kept everyone safe, every single day, just as he had promised his father.

*(pause)*

**Tonight's lesson:** A good big brother or sister looks after the little ones, and family takes care of each other, even on sad days.

**A question for you:** Who looks after you? And who can you look after?

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
