Kunti's Wonderful Prayer

Kids Mahabharath · Episode 12

For the grown-up: read slowly. Bold words are for acting out; · · · means a gentle pause. Pictures wait until the end!

Last time, King Pandu shot a deer in the forest by mistake. The deer was really Sage Kindama, who said Pandu would not have children in the usual way. Pandu said sorry, gave his crown to his brother, and went to live simply in a mountain hut with Kunti and Madri.

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight, something wonderful happens in the forest.

Every evening, Pandu sat outside the little hut and looked at the stars. Twinkle, twinkle. "I wish I had children," he sighed. "Who will play with me, and learn from me?"

Kunti held his hand. Then she smiled a secret smile.

· · ·

"My king," she said softly, "when I was a girl, I looked after Sage Durvasa. He gave me a special prayer. With it, I can call any god, and he will bless me with a baby."

Pandu's eyes went wide! Can you make big, surprised eyes, like Pandu? "Kunti, that is wonderful!" he said. "Let us call the god of goodness and truth first."

· · · · ·

So Kunti closed her eyes, folded her hands, and whispered the secret prayer. Shhh... very quietly.

A soft, shining light filled the forest. And there stood Dharma, the god of goodness and truth. He blessed Kunti, and soon a baby boy was born!

A voice from the sky said, "This child will always be good, honest and fair." They named him Yudhishthira.

· · ·

A while later, Pandu said, "Our family needs someone very, very strong. Let us call the god of the wind."

Kunti said the prayer again. Whoooosh! The trees bent, the leaves danced, and Vayu, the wind god, came swirling down. Soon, a second baby boy was born. They named him Bhima.

Can you make a strong arm, like Bhima? Show me your muscles!

· · ·

Baby Bhima was so strong! One day, Kunti was holding him on a big rock, when a tiger roared far away. Grrrrr! Kunti jumped up, and baby Bhima slipped and fell onto the rock.

Crack! Oh no! Was the baby hurt?

· · · · ·

No! The baby was fine, and giggling. It was the rock that broke into little pieces! Kunti and Pandu laughed and laughed. "What a strong little boy!"

· · ·

Then Pandu said, "Now let us call the king of the gods, Indra, for a brave hero."

Kunti said the prayer one more time. Boom! Rumble, rumble! Thunder rolled in the sky, and Indra came riding his great white elephant. Soon, a third baby boy was born, with bright, shining eyes. They named him Arjuna.

The voice from the sky said, "This child will be the greatest archer in the whole world!"

· · ·

The sages of the forest came to see the babies. Birds sang, tweet, tweet! and deer peeped from behind the trees.

Pandu held his three little sons, and his sad heart was full of joy at last. Kunti hugged them all. And gentle Madri smiled too, but she was wishing for a baby of her own.

· · ·

Tonight's lesson: Kunti shared her secret to make her family happy. When we share kindly, happiness grows bigger for everyone.

A question for you: Which baby would you like to be: good Yudhishthira, strong Bhima, or brave Arjuna?

Next time, Madri gets a turn with the wonderful prayer, and far away in Hastinapura, Gandhari's babies arrive.

Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams. 🌙
