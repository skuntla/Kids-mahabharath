# Episode 4: The Three Princesses of Kashi

*Last time, King Chitrangada did not come home, and gentle Vichitravirya became the boy-king. Big brother Bhishma promised to stand beside him and protect him.*

Snuggle in, little one. Let's see what happened next.

Little King Vichitravirya grew up, tall and kind. One day Queen Satyavati said, "Bhishma, our king is grown now. It is time for him to marry."

Just then, a messenger came running, **puff, puff, puff!** "News from the city of **Kashi**!" he said. "The King of Kashi has three daughters: Princess **Amba**, Princess **Ambika**, and Princess **Ambalika**. He is holding a **swayamvara**!"

*(pause)*

Do you know what a swayamvara is? It is a big, grand gathering where princes and kings from far and wide come, and a princess chooses who she will marry.

"I will go to Kashi for my brother," said Bhishma. He climbed into his chariot, and off he went, **clip-clop, clip-clop, rumble, rumble!**

*(pause)*

The hall in Kashi was full of kings in shining crowns. Flags were waving. Drums were going **dum, dum, dum!** And there stood the three princesses, holding flower garlands.

Bhishma stepped forward and said in a big, **booming voice**: "In the old warrior way, a brave hero may carry the brides away in his chariot. I take these three princesses to be queens for my brother, King Vichitravirya! If anyone wants to stop me, come and try!"

He helped the princesses into his chariot, and **whoosh**, away they went!

*(pause)*

All the kings jumped up. "Stop him!" they shouted. They chased after him with bows and arrows. **Twang, twang, twang!** Arrows flew like rain.

But Bhishma was the greatest archer in all the land. **Pull the string, and let go. Whoosh!** His arrows knocked their arrows right out of the sky! One by one, the kings gave up and went home.

*(pause)*

But one king did not give up. His name was **King Shalva**. "Stop, Bhishma!" he roared. "Fight me!"

They fought, **swish, swish, clang!** And Bhishma won. But Bhishma did not hurt him. He let King Shalva go home safely. A true hero is strong, and also kind.

*(long pause)*

Bhishma drove the chariot all the way home to Hastinapura. Queen Satyavati welcomed the princesses with flowers and lamps. Everything was ready for a wedding.

But the eldest princess, Amba, looked sad. She came up to Bhishma and said softly, "Great Bhishma, before you came, I had already chosen King Shalva in my heart. Please, may I go to him?"

*(pause)*

Bhishma listened carefully. He did not get angry. "Thank you for telling me the truth, Princess," he said gently. "You may go." And he sent her to King Shalva with helpers to keep her safe.

So Princess Ambika and Princess Ambalika married King Vichitravirya. There was music and dancing, and flowers everywhere. And Queen Satyavati smiled a happy smile.

*(pause)*

**Tonight's lesson:** Being strong is good, but being kind and listening to how others feel is even better.

**A question for you:** When someone tells you how they feel, how can you be a good listener?

What will happen to Princess Amba? We will find out next time.
