# Episode 22: Karna, King of Anga

*Last time, the princes showed their skills in a giant arena, and Arjuna's arrows made fire, rain and wind. Then a shining young man with golden armour clapped his arms at the gate, and Kunti held her breath.*

Snuggle in, little one. Tonight a stranger gets a golden crown!

The shining young man walked into the arena. Everyone whispered, "Who is he?"

He bowed to Guru Drona and Guru Kripa. Then he looked at Arjuna and said in a big, deep voice, **"Arjuna, everything you did, I can do too!"**

*(pause)*

Drona nodded. "Show us."

**Twang!** A fire arrow. **Twang!** A rain arrow. **Whoooosh!** A wind arrow. Then twenty-one arrows into the swinging horn, **twang-twang-twang!** He did every single thing Arjuna had done.

Duryodhana jumped up and hugged him. "What a hero! Be my friend!"

"I want only one thing," said the young man. "To have a contest with Arjuna, one to one." Arjuna's face grew hot. He lifted his bow.

*(pause)*

But Guru Kripa raised his hand. "Wait. In a princes' contest, both must be princes. Arjuna is a prince of the Kuru family. Tell us, young man, who is your father? Which family are you from?"

The young man was quiet. His head went down, like a flower in the rain. He was not a prince. Can you hang your head down, sad and quiet, like him?

*(long pause)*

Then Duryodhana stood up tall. "If only a king can fight Arjuna, I will make him a king! I give him the land of **Anga** to rule!"

The priests came running. They poured holy water on the young man's head, **splish, splash**. They put a golden crown on his head and held a white umbrella over him. Everyone clapped. Can you put a crown on your own head? **There!**

*(pause)*

Just then, an old man came hobbling in, leaning on a stick. It was **Adhiratha**, the chariot driver, the kind man who had found a baby in a basket floating down the river.

The new king saw him and jumped down. He bowed and touched the old man's feet, crown and all. Adhiratha hugged him and cried happy tears. "My son! My **Karna**!"

So now everyone knew. The golden young man was **Karna**, the son of a chariot driver.

*(pause)*

Bhima laughed. "A chariot driver's son? Go hold the horse whip instead of a bow!" That was a mean thing to say. Karna's lips trembled, and he looked up at the sun.

Duryodhana stood beside him. "Being great is about what you can do, not who your father is! Karna is my friend, and he is King of Anga!"

Karna held Duryodhana's hand. "You stood up for me today. I will be your friend forever." And he kept that promise all his life.

*(long pause)*

Across the arena, Kunti watched her secret son wearing his crown. Her head spun, and she fainted. Vidura gently sprinkled cool water on her face, and she woke up. But she still did not say a word.

The sun went down, and the great show was over. The Pandavas went home with Drona, and Duryodhana went home with Karna, his new best friend.

*(long pause)*

**Tonight's lesson:** Making fun of someone because of their family hurts their heart. A person is great because of how they act and what they learn, not who their parents are.

**A question for you:** If someone laughed at your friend, what would you say to help them?
