Meet the characters

The royal family

King ShantanuGangaDevavrata (Bhishma)

King Shantanu: the kind king of Hastinapura. (Episodes 1, 2, 3)
Ganga: the goddess of the river. (Episode 1)
Devavrata: their brave son. After his great promise he is called Bhishma. (Episodes 1, 2, 3)

By the river

SatyavatiFisherman Chief Dashraj

Satyavati: a kind fisherman's daughter who rows a flower-scented boat; she marries King Shantanu and becomes queen. (Episodes 2, 3)
Fisherman Chief Dashraj: Satyavati's father. (Episode 2)

The two princes

ChitrangadaVichitravirya

Chitrangada: the older son of King Shantanu and Satyavati, bold and brave; he becomes king. (Episode 3)
Vichitravirya: the gentle younger son, who becomes king as a boy while big brother Bhishma protects the kingdom. (Episode 3)
