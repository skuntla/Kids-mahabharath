Meet the characters

The royal family

King ShantanuGangaDevavrata (Bhishma)

King Shantanu: the kind king of Hastinapura. (Episodes 1, 2, 3)
Ganga: the goddess of the river. (Episodes 1, 5)
Devavrata: their brave son. After his great promise he is called Bhishma. (Episodes 1–7)

By the river

SatyavatiFisherman Chief Dashraj

Satyavati: a kind fisherman's daughter who rows a flower-scented boat; she marries King Shantanu and becomes queen. (Episodes 2, 3, 4)
Fisherman Chief Dashraj: Satyavati's father. (Episode 2)

The two princes

ChitrangadaVichitravirya

Chitrangada: the older son of King Shantanu and Satyavati, bold and brave; he becomes king. (Episode 3)
Vichitravirya: the gentle younger son, who becomes king as a boy while big brother Bhishma protects the kingdom. He grows up and marries Princess Ambika and Princess Ambalika. (Episodes 3, 4, 7)

The princesses of Kashi

AmbaAmbikaAmbalika

Amba: the eldest princess of Kashi, thoughtful and brave; she never gives up. In Episode 6 Lord Shiva grants her wish, and she is born again as Shikhandi. (Episodes 4, 5, 6)
Ambika: the cheerful middle princess; she marries King Vichitravirya, and becomes the mother of Dhritarashtra. (Episodes 4, 7)
Ambalika: the shy youngest princess; she also marries King Vichitravirya, and becomes the mother of Pandu. (Episodes 4, 7)

Two kings

King of KashiKing Shalva

King of Kashi: the father of the three princesses. (Episode 4)
King Shalva: a proud king who fights Bhishma; Bhishma lets him go safely. Later he refuses to marry Amba. (Episodes 4, 5)

Two wise sages

ParashuramaHotravahana

Parashurama: a mighty sage with an axe and a great bow; he was Bhishma's teacher. (Episode 5)
Hotravahana: Princess Amba's gentle grandfather, who lives in the forest. (Episode 5)

Lord Shiva, King Drupada and Shikhandi

Lord ShivaKing DrupadaShikhandi

Lord Shiva: the great god of the mountains, with a moon in his hair and a snake around his neck; he grants Amba's wish. (Episode 6)
King Drupada: the good king of Panchala, who prays for a child. (Episode 6)
Shikhandi: King Drupada's brave child, born carrying a little of Amba's heart; loves horses and archery. (Episode 6)

Sage Vyasa and the three princes

Sage VyasaDhritarashtraPanduVidura

Sage Vyasa: Queen Satyavati's wise son from long ago, a forest sage who blesses the family with children (he is also the one who first told the whole Mahabharata!). (Episode 7)
Dhritarashtra: Queen Ambika's son, big and as strong as a hundred elephants, born blind. (Episode 7)
Pandu: Queen Ambalika's son, with pale skin; a wonderful archer. (Episode 7)
Vidura: the kind palace helper's son, the wisest of all. (Episode 7)

Gandhari, Shakuni and King Subala

GandhariShakuniKing Subala

Gandhari: the gentle princess of Gandhara, blessed by Lord Shiva; she ties a cloth over her eyes to share everything with her husband, Dhritarashtra. (Episode 8)
Shakuni: Gandhari's very clever brother, who loves his sister and brings her to Hastinapura. (Episode 8)
King Subala: Gandhari and Shakuni's father, king of the mountain land of Gandhara. (Episode 8)
In Episode 8 the three princes are grown up, and Pandu becomes king.
