# Character guide (keep every episode consistent)

Reference sheet: `character-sheet.jpg` (left to right: Shantanu, Ganga, Devavrata).

**Art style:** soft watercolor storybook illustration, warm gentle light, cream/parchment tones, fine gold detailing, friendly expressions, suitable for young children.

## King Shantanu
- Adult king, warm brown skin, kind eyes
- Short black beard and moustache, wavy black hair
- Tall golden crown with jewels
- Saffron and gold robes with a flowing saffron shawl, gold necklaces and armbands, sandals

## Ganga (the river goddess)
- Light blue skin, serene smile
- Very long, flowing blue-black hair
- Sea-green / teal sari with gold borders, translucent and water-like
- Small lotus crown, gold jewelry, often barefoot near water

## Devavrata / Bhishma (as a teen)
- Teenage prince, warm brown skin, curly black hair
- Gold headband
- White dhoti with gold trim, purple sash across the chest, teal/sea-green shawl
- Carries a tall curved wooden bow; gold armbands, sandals
- After his vow he is called Bhishma (as an older man in later episodes, keep the same face and colors with white hair and beard)
