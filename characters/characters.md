## King Shantanu
- Reference: `sheet-1-shantanu-ganga-devavrata.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 1, Episode 2, Episode 3
- In Episode 3 he grows very old: same face and crown, with white hair and a long white beard
- Adult king, warm brown skin, kind eyes; short black beard and moustache, wavy black hair
- Tall golden crown with jewels; saffron and gold robes with a flowing saffron shawl, gold necklaces and armbands, sandals

## Ganga (the river goddess)
- Reference: `sheet-1-shantanu-ganga-devavrata.jpg` (middle)
- Appears in: Episode 1 (mentioned in Episode 2's recap), Episode 5 (stops the fight between Bhishma and Parashurama)
- Light blue skin, serene smile; very long flowing blue-black hair
- Sea-green / teal translucent sari with gold borders; small lotus crown; gold jewelry; often barefoot near water

## Devavrata / Bhishma
- Reference: `sheet-1-shantanu-ganga-devavrata.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 1 (as Devavrata), Episode 2 onward (Episode 6: looks at the stars on the palace roof, remembering Amba)
- **Called Devavrata in Episode 1; called Bhishma from Episode 2 on** (after his great promise)
- Teen/young man, warm brown skin, curly black hair, gold headband
- White dhoti with gold trim, purple sash across the chest, teal/sea-green shawl; tall curved wooden bow; gold armbands, sandals
- In later episodes as an older man: same face and colors, with white hair and beard

## Satyavati
- Reference: `sheet-2-satyavati-dashraj.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 2 onward (Queen Satyavati from Episode 3; Episode 7 calls her first son Vyasa)
- Kind young woman, a fisherman's daughter who rows a boat across the river; her presence smells of flowers
- Long dark braid with white flowers; coral-red and cream sari with gold border; gold earrings and bangles; wooden oar

## Fisherman Chief Dashraj
- Reference: `sheet-2-satyavati-dashraj.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 2
- Older man, warm brown skin, grey beard, white turban
- Brown kurta with a blue shawl over one shoulder, white dhoti, sandals; carries a fishing net

## Chitrangada
- Reference: `sheet-3-chitrangada-vichitravirya.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 3
- Older son of King Shantanu and Satyavati; bold, brave, loves adventures. Becomes king after Shantanu; does not return from his battle with a Gandharva (sky-warrior) of the same name
- Warm brown skin, short straight black hair, confident smile; small gold crown; deep red and gold tunic-dhoti with a maroon shawl, gold armbands; small sword at his waist. Shown as a boy (~12) and a young man (~18)

## Vichitravirya
- Reference: `sheet-3-chitrangada-vichitravirya.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 3, Episode 4, Episode 7 (falls sick and passes away gently, without children)
- In Episode 4 he is a grown young man (~20): same face, curly hair, peacock feather and colors, with a gold crown
- Younger son of King Shantanu and Satyavati; gentle, loves stories; becomes the boy-king, with Bhishma protecting the kingdom for him
- Warm brown skin, curly black hair with a small peacock feather (and a small gold crown once king); light blue and cream dhoti with gold trim and a cream shawl; gold necklace; small toy bow

## Amba
- Reference: `sheet-4-amba-ambika-ambalika.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 4, Episode 5 (Shalva and Bhishma both turn her away; she goes to the forest, then to Parashurama, then sets off to pray to Shiva), Episode 6 (prays in the snowy mountains; Shiva grants her wish; her spirit rises to be born again as Shikhandi)
- Eldest princess of Kashi, thoughtful and brave; had already chosen King Shalva in her heart
- Warm brown skin, tall, serious kind eyes; long black hair in a single braid with red roses; deep crimson-red sari with gold border; gold jhumka earrings and small gold tiara

## Ambika
- Reference: `sheet-4-amba-ambika-ambalika.jpg` (middle)
- Appears in: Episode 4 onward; marries King Vichitravirya; mother of Dhritarashtra (Episode 7: shuts her eyes when she sees Sage Vyasa)
- Middle princess, cheerful; warm brown skin, round face, black hair in a bun with jasmine; marigold-yellow sari with green border; gold tiara and bangles

## Ambalika
- Reference: `sheet-4-amba-ambika-ambalika.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 4 onward; marries King Vichitravirya; mother of Pandu (Episode 7: turns pale when she sees Sage Vyasa)
- Youngest princess, shy and sweet; lighter warm skin, short side-swept black hair with a lotus flower; soft lavender-pink sari with silver border; small silver tiara

## King of Kashi
- Reference: `sheet-5-king-of-kashi-shalva.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 4
- Father of the three princesses; older, plump, friendly; grey moustache, round gold crown; green and gold robes with a white shawl

## King Shalva
- Reference: `sheet-5-king-of-kashi-shalva.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 4, Episode 5 (refuses to marry Amba)
- Proud young king of Saubha; warm brown skin, thin black moustache, pointed silver crown; dark blue armor-tunic with silver trim, blue cape; sword and bow

## Gandharva Chitrangada (sky-warrior)
- No sheet (appears once, Episode 3): a shining sky-being in silver-blue robes with long flowing silver hair and a halo of light, floating in clouds

## Sage Parashurama
- Reference: `sheet-6-parashurama-hotravahana.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 5
- Mighty warrior-sage and Bhishma's archery teacher (Guru); fights Bhishma for days on Amba's behalf until Ganga and the gods stop them
- Tall and strong, warm brown skin, long white beard, white hair in a top-knot, white ash lines and red dot on forehead; saffron-orange dhoti and shoulder cloth, rudraksha beads; axe (parashu) over the shoulder, quiver and big bow, sandals

## Sage Hotravahana
- Reference: `sheet-6-parashurama-hotravahana.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 5
- Princess Amba's grandfather (her mother's father), a gentle forest sage who sends her to Parashurama
- Small, elderly, bald with a little white hair at the sides, short white beard, round kind face; white dhoti and cream shawl; wooden walking staff and small copper water pot

## Lord Shiva
- Reference: `sheet-7-shiva-drupada-shikhandi.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 8 (blesses Gandhari with a hundred children); Episode 6 (appears to Amba in the snowy mountains and grants her wish)
- Calm, kind god; light blue skin, long dark matted hair in a top-knot with a silver crescent moon and a small stream of water, third-eye mark; friendly small cobra around his neck, rudraksha beads, tiger-skin-pattern wrap with red sash; silver trident (trishul) with a small damaru drum; barefoot. Shown glowing in soft blue light

## King Drupada
- Reference: `sheet-7-shiva-drupada-shikhandi.jpg` (middle)
- Appears in: Episode 6 onward
- Good king of Panchala; father of Shikhandi (and later Draupadi and Dhrishtadyumna)
- Middle-aged, warm brown skin, neat black beard with a little grey, kind eyes; tall gold crown with a red ruby; maroon and gold robes with a mustard-yellow shawl; gold necklaces; sandals. His queen (no sheet): ivory and gold sari with head veil

## Shikhandi
- Reference: `sheet-7-shiva-drupada-shikhandi.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 6 onward
- Amba born again as King Drupada's child; brave, loves horses and archery. Will face Bhishma in the great battle far in the future (Shiva's boon). Avoid gendered pronouns in the kids' text for now (use "Shikhandi")
- Shown as a child (~10): warm brown skin, determined eyes, black hair in a high top-knot with a small red ribbon; forest-green and gold tunic with red sash, cream dhoti; leather arm guard; small wooden bow and quiver; sandals

## Sage Vyasa (Veda Vyasa)
- Reference: `sheet-8-vyasa-dhritarashtra-pandu-vidura.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 7 onward
- Satyavati's first son (born long before she married Shantanu); great forest sage who later tells the whole Mahabharata. Blesses the family so the princes are born
- Tall, dark brown skin, long tangled dark hair in a loose top-knot, big bushy dark beard, white ash marks on forehead; ochre spotted deer-skin wrap and cream shoulder cloth; rudraksha beads; wooden staff and palm-leaf manuscript bundle; barefoot

## Dhritarashtra
- Reference: `sheet-8-vyasa-dhritarashtra-pandu-vidura.jpg` (second from left)
- Appears in: Episode 7 onward
- Episode 8: grown up (~20), same face/colors, eyes closed; marries Gandhari; not made king because he cannot see. Reference for grown look: `episodes/ep08/img/1-practice.jpg`, `7-wedding.jpg`
- Son of Queen Ambika; born blind (eyes always gently closed), as strong as a hundred elephants
- Shown as a boy (~10): big, broad, warm brown skin, wavy black hair, small gold crown; royal blue and gold tunic with white dhoti, gold armbands. Later grown up: same face and colors

## Pandu
- Reference: `sheet-8-vyasa-dhritarashtra-pandu-vidura.jpg` (third from left)
- Appears in: Episode 7 onward
- Episode 8: grown up and crowned King Pandu (tall gold crown); reference `episodes/ep08/img/2-crown.jpg`
- Episode 11: shoots the deer (Sage Kindama), is told he cannot have children in the usual way, sends his crown to Dhritarashtra and lives simply in a forest hut with Kunti and Madri, no crown, plain white dhoti and sky-blue cloth (see `episodes/ep11/img/4-sorry.jpg`, `6-hut.jpg`)
- Episode 10: chosen by Kunti at her swayamvara, marries Madri too, wins many lands and shares the treasure; clean-shaven, curly black hair, pale ivory skin, white/silver dhoti with sky-blue sash, bow on shoulder (see `episodes/ep10/img/3-garland.jpg`, `6-share.jpg`)
- Son of Queen Ambalika; pale skin; a wonderful archer (future father of the Pandavas)
- Shown as a boy (~9): slim, noticeably pale ivory skin, straight black hair, small gold crown; white and silver tunic and dhoti with a sky-blue sash; small bow and quiver

## Vidura

- Episode 8: grown up, comforts Dhritarashtra at Pandu's crowning

## The kind palace maid (Vidura's mother)

## Gandhari

## Shakuni
