# Character guide (keep every episode consistent)

**Art style for all pictures:** soft watercolor storybook illustration, warm gentle light, cream/parchment tones, fine gold detailing, friendly expressions, suitable for young children.

When generating new pictures, attach the reference sheet(s) for every character in the scene, using these exact files:

## King Shantanu
- Reference: `sheet-1-shantanu-ganga-devavrata.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 1, Episode 2, Episode 3
- In Episode 3 he grows very old: same face and crown, with white hair and a long white beard
- Adult king, warm brown skin, kind eyes; short black beard and moustache, wavy black hair
- Tall golden crown with jewels; saffron and gold robes with a flowing saffron shawl, gold necklaces and armbands, sandals

## Ganga (the river goddess)
- Reference: `sheet-1-shantanu-ganga-devavrata.jpg` (middle)
- Appears in: Episode 1 (mentioned in Episode 2's recap)
- Light blue skin, serene smile; very long flowing blue-black hair
- Sea-green / teal translucent sari with gold borders; small lotus crown; gold jewelry; often barefoot near water

## Devavrata / Bhishma
- Reference: `sheet-1-shantanu-ganga-devavrata.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 1 (as Devavrata), Episode 2 onward
- **Called Devavrata in Episode 1; called Bhishma from Episode 2 on** (after his great promise)
- Teen/young man, warm brown skin, curly black hair, gold headband
- White dhoti with gold trim, purple sash across the chest, teal/sea-green shawl; tall curved wooden bow; gold armbands, sandals
- In later episodes as an older man: same face and colors, with white hair and beard

## Satyavati
- Reference: `sheet-2-satyavati-dashraj.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 2 onward (Queen Satyavati from Episode 3)
- Kind young woman, a fisherman's daughter who rows a boat across the river; her presence smells of flowers
- Long dark braid with white flowers; coral-red and cream sari with gold border; gold earrings and bangles; wooden oar

## Fisherman Chief Dashraj
- Reference: `sheet-2-satyavati-dashraj.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 2
- Older man, warm brown skin, grey beard, white turban
- Brown kurta with a blue shawl over one shoulder, white dhoti, sandals; carries a fishing net

## Chitrangada
- Reference: `sheet-3-chitrangada-vichitravirya.jpg` (left)
- Appears in: Episode 3
- Older son of King Shantanu and Satyavati; bold, brave, loves adventures. Becomes king after Shantanu; does not return from his battle with a Gandharva (sky-warrior) of the same name
- Warm brown skin, short straight black hair, confident smile; small gold crown; deep red and gold tunic-dhoti with a maroon shawl, gold armbands; small sword at his waist. Shown as a boy (~12) and a young man (~18)

## Vichitravirya
- Reference: `sheet-3-chitrangada-vichitravirya.jpg` (right)
- Appears in: Episode 3 onward
- Younger son of King Shantanu and Satyavati; gentle, loves stories; becomes the boy-king, with Bhishma protecting the kingdom for him
- Warm brown skin, curly black hair with a small peacock feather (and a small gold crown once king); light blue and cream dhoti with gold trim and a cream shawl; gold necklace; small toy bow

## Gandharva Chitrangada (sky-warrior)
- No sheet (appears once, Episode 3): a shining sky-being in silver-blue robes with long flowing silver hair and a halo of light, floating in clouds
